GC GameCars

Car models from racing games

Cars, rims and traffic vehicles extracted from the games below and converted to FBX: one file per car, Y up, metres, the front of the car towards +Z, textures inside the file (Real Racing 3: in a textures folder next to it). Where a game has them, hidden objects inside the FBX hold body kits, levels of detail and other variants. Every model is a separate download.

Shop / aftermarket rims only: the stock rim of every car is on the car itself. The Genki games (Kaido Racer 1 / 2, Racing Battle C1, TXR Drift) number their rims, so there stock and shop rims are shown together.
